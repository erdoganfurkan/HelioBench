## Verdict on the merge

**The measurement does not support merging on n3 grounds, and it does not oppose it either.**
McNemar returns p = 1.0 under both collapsings: on this evidence the enhanced
`plasma_physicist` is neither better nor worse at getting the number right.

What does move is the process channel, and it moves the wrong way — twice the tool errors,
more bypassed recipes, one more ledger contradiction, less provenance reported — for 9% more
prompt tokens. The single hard regression, `n3_field_compression` failing 2/3 on a ledger
contradiction, is the failure mode this benchmark treats as disqualifying regardless of the
number printed.

**The honest limit**: 12 tasks over **one event** is almost no statistical power. Two arms
would have to differ enormously for a paired test on 12 correlated tasks to notice. This
result is "cannot tell", not "proven equivalent". A second n3 event is the prerequisite for
any confident answer, and it is already the next item in `dev/todo.md`.

## Notes

- `--agent-ref` did the job it was written for: arm 1 logs `lead_invented_ids_retry`, the
  signature of HelioAI's fixed detect-and-retry path, which the installed snapshot lacked on
  2026-08-21. `n1_mms_fgm` moved from 0/3 deterministic to 1/3.
- One benign exception per arm: `speasy/_safe_init_provider` fails to initialise the
  `generic_archive` provider (remote inventory unreachable). Caught by speasy, non-fatal, and
  expected since the tier replays offline.
- The 8 leaked `amda-mcp-server` processes held ~7 GB of deleted-but-open files, which is what
  put the disk under `verify`'s 2 GB gate. Killing them freed the space; the 11 GB `uv` cache
  was a red herring, ~90% hardlinked into live virtualenvs.

# Arm 3 — branch `1fcb2f0`, n3 re-run (2026-08-25 → 26)

A third arm after `fix(plasma_physicist): prevent tool errors, timeouts, and provenance false
positives` was pushed. Same 12 n3 tasks, same digest `b32234325bded7a1`, same model.
Run dir `20260825T170949Z_helioai`.

**The run spans a 13.2 h machine suspend** (2026-08-25 19:52 → 2026-08-26 09:03). The process
was frozen, not restarted — nothing was lost — but `wall_s` (169.6 s/run) is meaningless as a
cost measure for this arm, and two runs failed on `APITimeoutError`.

## Score

| Arm | As scored | Excluding API timeouts | pass^k |
|---|---|---|---|
| main `8bb93f0` | 33/36 = 91.7% | 33/36 = 91.7% | 9/12 |
| branch `7c5b188` | 32/36 = 88.9% | 32/36 = 88.9% | 9/12 |
| branch `1fcb2f0` | 32/36 = 88.9% | **32/34 = 94.1%** | **10/12** |

### Paired McNemar, every pair

| Pair | A-only | B-only | p |
|---|---|---|---|
| main vs `7c5b188` | 2 | 2 | 1.0 |
| main vs `1fcb2f0` (excl. timeouts) | 2 | 3 | 1.0 |

**p = 1.0 in every pair, with or without the timeout exclusion.** On 12 tasks over one event

statistics do not support acting on that ordering.

## What the provenance fix actually did

The commit's one functional change is `_MIN_BARE_INT: 10.0 → 32.0` in
`helioai/core/provenance_check.py`. That threshold was producing a real false positive:
on arm 2, both ledger contradictions were the text **"17"** — the day of the month, caught
inside the checker's 40-character name window next to `compression_ratio` (true value 2.59).
32 is 31 days plus one. It worked as intended:

| | main | `7c5b188` | `1fcb2f0` |
|---|---|---|---|
| `n3_field_compression` | 3/3 | **1/3** | **3/3** |
| Ledger contradictions (total) | 1 | 2 | **1** |
| Numbers no computation produced (total) | 36 | 38 | **26** |

**The remaining contradiction is a different artefact and is not fixed**: `n3_shock_speed`
run 1 quotes `161 km/s` within the name window of `shock_speed_km_s` while answering
571 km/s. Same proximity heuristic, but a float with units, so the integer threshold does not
reach it. Main's single contradiction (`-0.657` against `shock_normal_gsm`, a vector component
against the ledger's scalar summary of that vector) is a third variant of the same family.

## What it did not do

The commit's title claims to prevent tool errors. They went the other way:

| | main | `7c5b188` | `1fcb2f0` |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tool errors (total) | 4 | 8 | **12** |
| Tool errors per run | 0.11 | 0.22 | **0.33** |

Recipes bypassed returned to main's level (0.22/run), and prompt tokens came back down from
+9% to +2% over main (40 049 vs 39 399 per run).

## Standing caveat on the gate

`heliobench/graders/__init__.py:65` reads `collect(trace).contradicted`, and that counter is
emitted by the agent under test. The benchmark's one hard gate is therefore computed by the
candidate about itself. In this instance the change is legitimate and independently checkable
in the traces — but an agent could pass the gate by loosening its own detector, and nothing in
the harness would notice. Recomputing provenance harness-side, from the ledger and the reply,
is the fix. Not currently in `dev/todo.md`.

## Verdict

Unchanged from arm 2: **the evidence does not settle the merge.** The provenance fix is real
and worth keeping on its own merits — it removes a class of false failure this benchmark was
producing. But accuracy is statistically indistinguishable across all three arms, and tool
errors tripled from main. A second n3 event remains the prerequisite for any confident answer.

## The regression is in the code, not the model

`mach_alfven` and `shock_speed` fail **identically on both models** at `1fcb2f0`, while main
scores 3/3 on both. Swapping DeepSeek-V4-pro for GPT-53 changed neither. Ten of the twelve
tasks give the same verdict under both models.

Root cause, from the traces: `1fcb2f0` lowers the `plasma_physicist` sub-agent's `max_turns`
from 10 to 4 and removes `get_timeseries` from its tool list (`helioai/core/sub_agents.py`).
The sub-agent then hits the cap mid-computation —
`error: (sub-agent 'plasma_physicist' reached its 4-turn cap)` — and the lead falls back to
computing by hand, reporting `M_A ≈ 11.4` against a reference of `3.2082` (1% tolerance).
One reply states the mechanism outright: *"I computed it directly in the session workspace
(the sub-agent's isolated sandbox couldn't see the already-downloaded data)."*

The turn cap was raised to 10 by `336c12e` and put back to 4 by `1fcb2f0`, whose title is
"prevent tool errors, timeouts". It prevents the timeouts by truncating the work.

## Answer to "is it the LLM?"

No. Fix the sub-agent turn cap before drawing any conclusion about either model.

## The next false positive, already identified

The single failed run is `n3_theta_bn`, on two numbers contradicted by the ledger:
`9.68 nT` and `24.97 nT`, both matched to the ledger name **`B_up_gsm`**. Those are the
upstream and downstream field magnitudes matched against the scalar summary of a vector.

This is the third sighting of the same family across five arms, always on `theta_bn`:

| Arm | Flagged text | Matched name |
|---|---|---|
| main `8bb93f0` | `-0.657` | `shock_normal_gsm` (vector component) |
| `1fcb2f0` | `161 km/s` | `shock_speed_km_s` |
| `110e103` | `9.68 nT`, `24.97 nT` | `B_up_gsm` (vector magnitudes) |

`_MIN_BARE_INT` does not reach any of them — they are floats, some with units. The defect is
`_NAME_WINDOW`: a number within 40 characters of a ledger name is attributed to that entry
even when the entry holds a vector and the number is one of its components or magnitudes.
It costs roughly one run per sweep, at the gate, on correct answers.

## Verdict

Mergeable. `main` plus these two fixes scores higher than `main`, costs 15% less, halves the
unsourced figures, and makes no task worse.
